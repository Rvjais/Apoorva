A little clarity

Good questions.
A clearer start.

A few things you might be wondering before you come in. For anything else, let’s talk.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?

No. You can start by sharing your concerns and questions. A consultation is a space to discuss the available options and understand what may be suitable for you.

Can I book a visit just for a skincare routine?

Yes. Personalised skincare routines are part of our services. Bring a list of the products you currently use and what you would like to discuss.

Do you offer both skin and hair services?

Yes. Our services include skin concerns, aesthetics, skin renewal, hair fall and hair loss, dandruff care, laser hair reduction, and wellness treatments. You can browse the complete list above.

What should I ask before a treatment?

Ask about suitability, what the treatment involves, potential risks, preparation, aftercare, costs, and follow-up. Bring up any medical history or concerns you would like to discuss with the clinician.

How do I arrange my first consultation?

Contact the clinic to discuss availability and arrange a visit. You can explore our treatment menu beforehand, or simply tell us what’s on your mind.
