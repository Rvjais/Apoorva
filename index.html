Patient FAQs

Have Questions?

A few things you might be wondering before your consultation. For anything else, call +91 98216 14611 or send an enquiry below.

Ask Us a Question↗
Do I need to know which treatment I want? +

No. Start by sharing your concerns and questions. Your consultation is a space to explore the available options and discuss what may be suitable for you.

Can I visit just for a skincare routine? +

Yes. Personalised skincare planning is part of our services. Bring a list of your current products and the concerns you would like to discuss.

Do you offer both skin and hair care? +

Yes. Explore our menu for skin concerns, aesthetic treatments, hair fall, scalp concerns, laser hair reduction, and wellness consultations.

What should I ask before a treatment? +

Ask about suitability, potential risks, preparation, aftercare, costs, and follow-up. Share any relevant medical history and previous treatments with Dr. Apurva.

How do I arrange my first consultation? +

Call +91 98216 14611 or email apurva.mini@gmail.com to discuss availability. The enquiry form below helps you prepare an email; appointments are confirmed directly with the clinic.
