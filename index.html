Patient FAQs

Have questions?

A few things you might be wondering before you come in. For anything else, let’s talk.

Do I need to know which treatment I want?

No. Start by sharing your concerns and questions. Your consultation is a space to explore the available options and discuss what may be suitable for you.

Can I visit just for a skincare routine?

Yes. Personalised skincare planning is part of our services. Bring a list of your current products and the concerns you’d like to discuss.

Do you offer both skin and hair care?

Yes. Explore our menu for skin concerns, aesthetic treatments, hair fall, scalp concerns, laser hair reduction, and wellness consultations.

What should I ask before a treatment?

Ask about suitability, potential risks, preparation, aftercare, costs, and follow-up. Share any relevant medical history and previous treatments with your clinician.

How do I arrange my first consultation?

Call +91 98216 14611 or email apurva.mini@gmail.com to discuss availability. The consultation enquiry button can help you prepare an email. An appointment is confirmed directly with the doctor.
